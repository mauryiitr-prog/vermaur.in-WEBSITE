About Vermaur Farms

Vermaur Farms is dedicated to bringing premium-quality rice, traditional food products, and authentic farm-fresh goodness directly from trusted Indian farms to households across the country.


Founded with a vision to preserve India's rich agricultural heritage, we focus on sourcing carefully selected grains and traditional products that deliver purity, nutrition, and exceptional taste.


Our product range includes premium varieties of rice such as Kala Namak Rice, Sona Masoori Rice and Doodh Malai Rice, along with traditional foods like Dal Badi and other farm-based products.


At Vermaur Farms, we believe that great food begins at the farm. By working closely with farmers and maintaining strict quality standards, we strive to deliver authentic flavors and trusted quality.
